## Project

**SERVO DEUTSCH B2** is an Android learning app for people preparing for the Goethe-Zertifikat B2. We are preparing a small listening-audio pilot: exam 001, consisting of eight short audio tracks across the four listening sections.

## Roles sought

We are looking for German-speaking volunteers for natural, clear recordings. Depending on the final scripts, roles may include:
- Standard German narrator / presenter
- Conversational speakers for short dialogues
- Additional voices for announcements or interview-style passages

You may audition for one or more roles. Native speakers are welcome, but clear and confident German pronunciation is the key requirement.

## What the volunteer task involves

- Record only the assigned script(s), not the entire exam.
- Use a quiet room and a phone or microphone capable of producing clean audio.
- Speak naturally and at a moderate, exam-appropriate pace.
- Follow the supplied pronunciation and file-naming notes.
- The pilot contains eight tracks in total; the exact number of lines per volunteer will be agreed in advance.
- There is no payment and no obligation to continue beyond the agreed pilot contribution.

## Audio and permissions

Before any final recording is accepted, each contributor will receive written terms explaining where and how the audio may be used, including in the app and downloadable listening packs, whether it may be edited for sound quality, the duration of permission, and how to raise a withdrawal request.

We will not clone voices, synthesize a contributor's voice, or use recordings to train AI voice models. Recordings will not be published before the contributor has agreed to the applicable terms.

## Credits

Each contributor may independently choose one option:
- Real name
- Pseudonym
- Anonymous / no public credit

Choosing a credit option is voluntary and does not affect selection.
